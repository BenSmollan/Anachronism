# Map data sources and licences

| File | What | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `europe_*` | Elevation (Terrarium tiles), satellite colour (NASA Blue Marble) and rivers (Natural Earth) for Europe, the Mediterranean and the Near East (11°W–62°E, 17°N–67°N) | Terrarium tiles, Mapzen / AWS Open Data Terrain Tiles (SRTM, GMTED2010, ETOPO1, others); NASA Blue Marble; Natural Earth 10 m rivers | Open (attribution required, see below); public domain (NASA, Natural Earth) |

Attribution to show in the game's credits:
"Elevation: Mapzen Terrain Tiles (SRTM, GMTED2010, ETOPO1 and others), via AWS Open Data.
Imagery: NASA Blue Marble. Rivers: Natural Earth."

Rebuild (see D-053): `client/tools/build_heightmap.gd`, `client/tools/build_colour.gd`,
`scripts/build_rivers.py`.

## Sounds and music (client/sounds/)
All generated by `scripts/make_sounds.py` from simple synthesis (plucked strings, gong,
drums, chimes, clicks). They are original to this project; no third-party audio is used.
